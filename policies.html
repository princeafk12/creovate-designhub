Terms & policies

Clear expectations for every order.

Payment

Full payment must be confirmed before any work starts.

Delivery

Delivery time counts from confirmed payment and a complete brief. Files are delivered by email or Google Drive in PDF and PNG.

Meeting the brief

We deliver what your approved brief describes. If it does not match, we correct it at no charge.

No revisions

No free revisions and no changes after delivery. Please give a clear, complete brief first.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel before delivery for a full refund within 5 working days. We also refund in full if we fail to deliver. After delivery, no refunds.

Ownership

Once paid in full, you own the final design. We may show it in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Privacy and previously saved reports

AI tools are temporarily unavailable and new AI tool payments are paused. If you used a tool before this pause, your saved result remains in your signed-in account. Some earlier briefs were processed by Google Gemini; Google states that content sent through its unpaid Gemini API services may be used to improve its products. Do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential business information or sensitive personal data. We collect only what we need to deliver orders, do not sell your data, and you can contact CREOVATE to request access, correction or deletion.
