Terms & policies

Clear expectations for every order.

Payment

Full payment must be confirmed before any work starts.

Delivery

Delivery time counts from confirmed payment and a complete brief. Files are delivered by email or Google Drive in PDF and PNG.

Meeting the brief

We deliver what your approved brief describes. If it does not match, we correct it at no charge.

No revisions

No free revisions and no changes after delivery. Please give a clear, complete brief first.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel before delivery for a full refund within 5 working days. We also refund in full if we fail to deliver. After delivery, no refunds.

Ownership

Once paid in full, you own the final design. We may show it in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Privacy and AI processing

We collect only what we need to deliver the service. Design briefs open in WhatsApp so we can respond. Business Tool and Validator prompts are sent to our configured AI provider, currently Google Gemini, and completed reports are saved to your signed-in account. Google states that free-tier Gemini API content may be used to improve its products. Do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential business information or sensitive personal data. The Validator also reads public country indicators from the World Bank. We do not sell your data; contact CREOVATE to request access, correction or deletion.
